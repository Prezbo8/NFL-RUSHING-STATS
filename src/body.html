Reading this page

  • Each block reads like the RUNNING MODEL tab — a stats row for the team with its league RANK directly underneath. Ranks are green in the top 10, red in the bottom 10. Totals, attempts and games carry no rank, same as the sheet.
  • Ranks know which way each stat runs. For a defense, rank 1 is the fewest yards allowed and the lowest DVOA — but the highest stuff rate, since forcing stuffs is the good outcome.
  • The chips under each block are percentile gaps between the offense and the defense facing it, so a rushing offense's DVOA and a defense's DVOA allowed become comparable. Positive favors the offense; under 8 points reads as even.
  • aYBCO + aYACO does not equal YPC here — FTN's contact splits and the statshub attempt counts cover different play universes, differing by about 0.9 yds/att. YPC is computed from attempts and yards.
  • In Rankings, the first click on a column sorts best-first and knows which way each stat runs — on defense, low DVOA and low yards allowed are good, while a high stuff rate is good. Click again to reverse. Shading tracks league percentile, so the strongest cells read darkest.
  • CRT% is omitted: it is exactly aYACO / (aYBCO + aYACO) and adds nothing. aRYOE is omitted because FTN has not populated it this season yet.
